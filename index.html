Powered by Claude

Built on Claude, because hiring is too important for a model that guesses.

Every Helio interview, translation and scoring rationale runs on Claude, Anthropic's family of AI models. We picked Claude for three things recruiting can't compromise on: nuance across languages, reasoning you can audit, and safety by design.

Multilingual nuance

Claude conducts the interview natively in each language rather than translating English on the fly, so idioms, politeness registers and code-switching such as Sheng, Hinglish or Taglish are understood rather than mangled.

Rubric reasoning

For each competency, Claude scores against your rubric and cites the exact transcript lines it relied on. Recruiters see why, not just a number.

Long context

The whole job description, rubric, company policies and conversation stay in context, so follow-up questions stay on-role and consistent for every candidate.

Safety by design

Anthropic's Constitutional AI training plus Helio's guardrails keep interviews respectful. The model never asks about age, religion, family status or other protected attributes.

Fairness & trust

Less bias than a tired human at applicant #400.

Structured interviews and game-based assessment are among the most predictive and least biased tools in hiring science. Helio makes them practical at volume, and audits itself continuously.

Same questions, every time

Every candidate for a role gets the same core interview and rubric, whatever their language, accent or time zone.

Adverse-impact monitoring

Pass rates are tracked by gender, age band and region on every requisition. Drift beyond the four-fifths rule triggers an alert and a human review.

Candidate-first data

Candidates see what was assessed and can request their data or a human review at any time. Their data is never used to train AI models.

Which AI model does Helio use?

Helio is built on Claude, the AI model family by Anthropic. Claude runs the interviews, translations, follow-up questions and rubric scoring. Enterprise customers can run Claude through AWS Bedrock or Google Cloud Vertex AI in their own cloud account.

Does the AI make hiring decisions?

No. Helio recommends and humans decide. Every shortlist is reviewed by your recruiters, low-confidence evaluations are routed to a person, and any candidate can request a human review.

How valid are the games?

Each game is adapted from an established paradigm from the research literature, such as BART, Go/No-Go, the Ultimatum Game and matrix reasoning. Before launch, each game goes through reliability testing and local norming per market.

Can candidates cheat by using AI themselves?

Interviews adapt follow-up questions to each answer and the games are time-based, so copy-pasting from a chatbot gets flagged quickly. Unusual patterns are surfaced to recruiters rather than auto-rejected.

How long does setup take?

A pilot role goes live in about three days. Upload a job description and Helio drafts the rubric and interview with Claude. Your team edits and approves it, and then you share the link.
